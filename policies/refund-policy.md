# Acme refund policy (customer support)

Agents and the copilot apply these rules exactly. When a rule does not settle a case, a person decides.

1. Change of mind: a refund is possible within 30 days of delivery, for items returned unused. The customer pays return shipping.
2. Damaged or broken on arrival: a full refund or a free replacement, without returning the item, when the customer sends a photo within 14 days of delivery.
3. Late parcel: when a parcel is more than 5 business days past its promised date, offer a 10 EUR voucher. A late parcel alone is not a refund.
4. Lost parcel: when tracking has not moved for 14 days, the parcel is lost; refund in full or ship a replacement, the customer's choice.
5. Final sale items and gift cards are never refunded.
6. Never promise a refund the rules above do not allow. Never ask for a card number or a password.
